# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data FitnessClub processes, why, on what legal basis, and what rights you have over it.

## 1. Data controller

The controller of your data is **Onda**, Av. Miguel Bombarda, 110, Lisbon, Portugal, tax number 910888961.

For any privacy question, write to [privacidade@onda.work](mailto:privacidade@onda.work).

## 2. Data we collect

- **Account:** name, email, password (stored only as an irreversible hash), language, theme, time zone, and the dates you signed up and accepted these documents.
- **Training:** programmes, workouts and exercises you create, sessions (start, end, notes), sets (weight, reps, time or distance, effort) and the records calculated from them.
- **Groups and planned workouts:** the groups you're in, and the workouts you plan or join (date, time, type and the place chosen in a search — gym, park or neighbourhood — never your GPS location). "Near me" uses your location only on your phone to centre the map; it isn't stored or sent.
- **Profile and community:** every account has a profile (@, name, bio, photo if you add one, whether you show strength levels), friendships, blocks, posts (text and the workout summary you choose to share), likes, comments, reports and notifications.
- **Training profile:** goals, level, equipment, focus muscles, training days and session length, and what you write in your requests and in what you want to avoid.
- **Artificial intelligence (with consent):** the programmes and weekly reviews created by AI, and the cost of each request.
- **Health data (with explicit consent):** weigh-ins and your weight goal (direction and pace).
- **Preferences:** default rest time, end-of-rest alert and keeping the screen on during workouts.
- **Notifications:** push notification subscriptions for your devices (the push service's technical address and encryption keys).
- **Minimal technical data:** IP address and browser information, used only for security and to limit abuse (for example, too many login attempts), and the days you opened the app (aggregated counts in the admin area).

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

- **Providing the service** (account, workouts, sessions, progress, rest alerts, export): performance of the contract you accept when creating your account.
- **Weight and goal** (health data): **explicit consent**, used solely to show you your weight, averages and goal. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings without affecting the rest of the app.
- **Artificial intelligence** (proposed programmes and the weekly review, on the Premium plan): **consent**, asked before first use and withdrawable at any time in Settings › Privacy without affecting the rest of the app.
- **Profile and community:** performance of the contract. Your profile (@, name, bio, photo, level and badges) is visible to people with a FitnessClub account (never outside the app); posting is always your choice and each post is seen by whoever you choose (public or friends only). Weight, goal and strength levels never show without your permission. Reports are seen only by moderators.
- **Security and abuse prevention** (attempt limits, technical logs): legitimate interest in protecting your account and the service.
- **Service emails** (email confirmation, password recovery, security notices): performance of the contract. We do not send marketing.

## 4. Artificial intelligence and sharing

AI is optional and only works with your consent. When you ask for a programme or get your weekly review, we send **Gemini** (Google) only your training profile and your workout numbers (sets, reps, loads, records and the names of exercises and workouts). **We never send** your name, email, body weight, weight goal or photos. For now we use Google's free API tier: under it, Google may use what it receives (without your name or email) to improve its products, including through human review. When we move to the paid tier, where this does not happen, we will update this policy.

AI text is an automatically generated suggestion: the numbers are calculated by the app and the text is checked against them, but it may contain mistakes. It is not medical advice.

We do not sell your data or share it with other people or companies beyond the processors below, who only handle it to provide the service.

## 5. Connecting to other apps

FitnessClub is independent: it has its own accounts and database. If a connection to another app (for example, MYGLOW) is ever offered, it will only be switched on with your explicit permission, and this policy will be updated first.

## 6. Processors

We use these providers, who process data only on our behalf and according to our instructions:

- **Vercel** — hosting for the app and the exercise images;
- **Neon** — database;
- **Inngest** — scheduled tasks (end-of-rest alert, AI requests);
- **OpenStreetMap** — map and place search for planned workouts: receives only the text you search for and, when showing the map, your device's IP address (like any website);
- **Google (Gemini API)** — artificial intelligence, only with your consent (see section 4);
- **Upstash** — usage limits (security);
- **Resend** — sending emails;
- **Sentry** — technical error logging, on servers in the EU, without your workout content or weight;
- **Vercel Web Analytics** — aggregated statistics for public pages, without cookies.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In those cases, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, namely the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

## 7. Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, **all your data is removed from the database immediately**. It may remain in encrypted backups for up to {{DIAS_BACKUP}} days, after which it is deleted.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access** and **portability** — in Settings › Privacy, "Export my data" downloads everything as a JSON file;
- **rectification** — you can edit your data in the app at any time;
- **erasure** — in Settings › Danger zone, "Delete account" removes everything;
- **withdraw consent** for AI and for health data, in Settings, without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing (you can also delete your weigh-ins and goal);
- **object to** and **restrict** processing, where the law allows;
- lodge a **complaint** with the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD) or the supervisory authority in your country.

To exercise any of these rights, write to [privacidade@onda.work](mailto:privacidade@onda.work).

## 9. Minimum age

FitnessClub is intended for people aged **16 and over**. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if we learn that this has happened, we delete the account.

## 10. Cookies

We only use **essential** cookies: your session (to keep you signed in), language and app preferences (for example, hiding a notice). We do not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the version at the top and ask you to accept it again in the app before you continue.
