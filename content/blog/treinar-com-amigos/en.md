The best programme in the world is useless if you drop it after three weeks. And what helps most to keep going isn't willpower: it's having someone waiting for you.

## The problem with ordinary leaderboards

Most apps compare who lifts the most. That puts off beginners, who'll never be at the top, and it mostly measures genetics and years of training.

## Leagues by level, weeks that reset

In FitnessClub, the ranking works differently:

- **You compete with people at a similar stage.** Everyone sits in the league for their level: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum or Diamond.
- **This week's effort counts**, not strength. XP comes from workouts, sets, personal records, activities and hitting your weekly goal, with caps so nobody wins by doing 200 sets in a day.
- **Every week starts from zero.** Had a bad week? There's a fresh start on Sunday.
- **The top 3 in each league earn a win** that stays on their profile.

## Community matters

- **A feed** with workouts from friends and the community, with likes and comments.
- **Groups** with a shared weekly goal (workouts, minutes or XP).
- **Workout invites**, with the place on a map.
- **Carousel posts**: your photo on the cover and the workout, day or achievement on the next slide.

## Private by default

Your weight is yours alone. Posts can be friends-only, and you can leave the ranking whenever you like.
